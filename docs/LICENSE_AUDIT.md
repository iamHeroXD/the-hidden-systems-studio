## 2. Audio & Media Asset Licensing Guidelines
1. **Background Music & Sound Effects**:
   - Only use public domain (CC0), royalty-free procedural audio generators, or explicitly licensed music.
   - The Studio includes a procedural ambient synth/drone generator using FFmpeg audio synthesis filter expressions (`sine`, `anoisesrc`, `lowpass`, `flanger`) which are 100% royalty-free and copyright safe.
2. **Narration Audio**:
   - Synthesized voices generated by Kokoro-ONNX (Apache-2.0) and Edge-TTS carry no commercial restrictions on generated waveforms.
3. **Illustrations & Vector Graphics**:
   - Generated SVGs, character rigs, and Rough.js procedural visuals are generated entirely by the studio algorithms; full copyright belongs to the operator.
4. **YouTube Data Usage**:
   - Public scraping and yt-dlp metadata extraction are restricted to competitive market intelligence and research, strictly adhering to Fair Use.

## 3. Human Approval & Release Gate
- The studio **never** automatically uploads or distributes videos to YouTube without explicit manual operator approval.
- All generated productions remain in local staging until inspected and approved via the Studio Dashboard.
